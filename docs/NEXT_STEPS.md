The bots' end-of-turn discards are a simple keep-the-best-5 heuristic, not tuned.

## Smaller open items

- Bot card play was reviewed (`--card-stats`, 600 runs, seeds 1 to 3). Fixed: Borrowed Time and Seance were almost never played (the energy-refunding draw cards ranked last in greedy and were scored as a full-cost card in smart); Reckoning was played at 0 damage with no Haunted enemy and aimed at the lowest-HP enemy; Spirit Tithe was played with nothing to heal. Plays per run, before to after: Borrowed Time 0.19 to 1.12 (greedy) and 0.21 to 0.84 (smart), Seance 0.04 to 0.10 and 0.26 to 1.11, Reckoning (greedy) 1.75 to 0.45 because the wasted 0-damage plays are gone. Still rarely used, and judged genuinely weak rather than mis-played: Many Hands (2 cost, 2 damage per play; best case about 6 damage for 2 energy against Shove's 6 for 1, and holding it to the end of the turn starved it of energy, cutting Last Keepsake's plays from 10 to 4); Reckoning and Spirit Tithe for the smart bot (never bought: they score below the deck average, and Tithe needs a Haunted enemy plus missing HP); Seance and Spirit Tithe for the greedy bot (it only takes damage and block cards). Update (after v0.8.0): Many Hands was since retuned to Deal 3 x3 (9 for 2 energy, level with Shove per energy) and is left alone. Reckoning is now Deal 3x Haunt and Spirit Tithe Heal 4 per Haunted enemy; with 300 runs each, the smart bot now keeps Reckoning in 16% of Medium decks and plays it 3.3 times per run when held (it never did before), and Medium escape did not move (smart 70.7 to 68.7, greedy 44.0 to 44.0). Both still want a human look.
- A sim-bot card that refunds its own cost and draws (Borrowed Time, Seance) is skipped when the draw pile holds fewer cards than it draws, since it would redraw itself and loop to the per-turn play cap.
- Save-scumming (quit mid-fight, Continue replays the stop) is intentional.
- Economy (gold, vendor prices) is still untested beyond a back-of-envelope check.
- Relics: playtest the feel and prices (Brass Token Purse is the bots' favorite at 50 gold); the bots only buy relics and do not play around them, so sim numbers understate them. The barbed-wire badge reads closer to a chain. Tooltips have not been seen in a real run. More relic sources (elites, a free pick after a fight) are an option.
- Bigger ideas: a branching map, more enemies and an elite tier (which could also drop relics).

## How to run

- Play: open the project in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5 (or launch `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .`).
- Full check: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/check.ps1`
- Sim: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tools/run_sim.gd -- --runs 600 --seed 1 --character deserter --policy smart --card-stats`
