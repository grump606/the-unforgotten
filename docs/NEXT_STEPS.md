## Smaller open items

- Bot card play was reviewed (`--card-stats`, 600 runs, seeds 1 to 3). Fixed: Pocket Watch and Seance were almost never played (the energy-refunding draw cards ranked last in greedy and were scored as a full-cost card in smart); Reckoning was played at 0 damage with no Haunted enemy and aimed at the lowest-HP enemy; Spirit Tithe was played with nothing to heal. Plays per run, before to after: Pocket Watch 0.19 to 1.12 (greedy) and 0.21 to 0.84 (smart), Seance 0.04 to 0.10 and 0.26 to 1.11, Reckoning (greedy) 1.75 to 0.45 because the wasted 0-damage plays are gone. Still rarely used, and judged genuinely weak rather than mis-played: Spree (2 cost, 2 damage per play; best case about 6 damage for 2 energy against Shove's 6 for 1, and holding it to the end of the turn starved it of energy, cutting Last Pocket's plays from 10 to 4); Reckoning and Spirit Tithe for the smart bot (never bought: they score below the deck average, and Tithe needs a Haunted enemy plus missing HP); Seance and Spirit Tithe for the greedy bot (it only takes damage and block cards). Worth a human look at Spree, Spirit Tithe and Reckoning.
- A sim-bot card that refunds its own cost and draws (Pocket Watch, Seance) is skipped when the draw pile holds fewer cards than it draws, since it would redraw itself and loop to the per-turn play cap.
- Save-scumming (quit mid-fight, Continue replays the stop) is intentional.
- Economy (gold, vendor prices) is still untested beyond a back-of-envelope check.
- Relics: playtest the feel and prices (Brass Token Purse is the bots' favorite at 50 gold); the bots only buy relics and do not play around them, so sim numbers understate them. The barbed-wire badge reads closer to a chain. Tooltips have not been seen in a real run. More relic sources (elites, a free pick after a fight) are an option.
- Bigger ideas: a branching map, more enemies and an elite tier (which could also drop relics).

## How to run

- Play: open the project in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5 (or launch `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .`).
- Full check: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/check.ps1`
- Sim: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tools/run_sim.gd -- --runs 600 --seed 1 --character deserter --policy smart --card-stats`
